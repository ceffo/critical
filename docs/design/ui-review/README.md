# UI/UX review: colours, contrast, hierarchy and consistency (2026-10-17)

An autonomous critique of the real app at the two layout viewports (1707x1300, the user's table
screen; 1440x900), in every colour theme, in English and French. The user's brief: _"the UI is still
unsatisfactory for me. especially colors, contrast. things don't pop, sections are hard to see.
there are inconsistencies everywhere ... review the colour themes ... currently one or two of them
are usable the rest is garbage ... come up with a few recommendations."_ The recommendations at the
end are briefs for a mock-up round; the user picks one.

- **Screenshots**: `shots/` holds the curated set listed in [`curated.txt`](curated.txt), named
  `<mode>-<ambiance>[-fr]--<screen>-<WxH>.png`. `just ui-shots` regenerates everything (about 230
  files in the gitignored `web/e2e-results/ui-review/`) and re-copies the curated ones.
- **Measurements**: [`contrast.md`](contrast.md), the WCAG ratios of every token pair in every
  ambiance and mode, generated by the same command from `web/src/lib/theme.ts`.
- **Mock-ups**: [`mockups/`](mockups/README.md) builds the four options of "Recommendations" over the
  real app, beside today's screens, with each option's theme set measured (`just ui-mockups`).
- **Capture**: `web/ui-review/screens.spec.ts` and `contrast.spec.ts` on their own Playwright config
  (`web/playwright.ui-review.config.ts`), on the e2e stack (in-memory API on a free port, the
  `api/testdata` fixture; never ports 8080/5173/5432 or `.dev/`). Not part of `just validate` nor of
  `just e2e`.

## The short version: five root causes

Every per-screen complaint below traces to one of these. Fix the causes and most screens fix
themselves.

1. **The surfaces do not separate.** Measured (dark Arcane): card on page 1.10:1, raised step
   (`neu-900`) on card 1.19:1, the card outline (`ink` at 18 %) 1.56:1 against the page, the result
   card's "accent" outline (`amb-700`) 2.49:1. Light mode is no better (1.18 / 1.08 / 1.45 / 1.59).
   WCAG asks 3:1 for a boundary that carries meaning. The cards of ADR 0022 step 4.1 exist in the
   DOM, not on the screen: _"sections are hard to see"_ is literally true.
2. **Every accent sits at the same lightness.** In dark mode `tint`, `highlight`, `magic`, `danger`,
   `success`, `warning` are all drawn at L 0.72–0.82; danger vs tint is **1.03:1**, success vs tint
   1.36:1, highlight vs tint 1.46:1 (light mode: 1.26 / 1.35 / 1.44). They differ by hue only, so
   nothing stands out from anything: a crit, a hit, a damage value and a button all have the same
   visual weight. _"Things don't pop"_ is this row of the table.
3. **The primary hue is wallpaper.** The ambiance hue paints the HP bar when healthy, the effect
   pills, the current-turn tint, the selected journal row, the rail pill, the initiative chip, the
   weapon-in-hand card, the result outline, the ammunition chips, the scrollbars, the level
   underline and every primary button. When one colour means "chrome", "selected", "healthy",
   "current", "in hand" and "latest" at once, it means nothing, and a theme change recolours the
   whole screen instead of its accents (compare `dark-grove`, `dark-blood`).
4. **Muted is the default voice.** `text-muted-foreground` appears 168 times in `web/src/components`,
   `text-foreground` 28 times. Stats lines, meta lines, column headers, placeholders, the whole
   journal's time column, tab captions and most labels are at 70 % ink. The eye has nothing to rest
   on because almost everything is secondary.
5. **Three of six ambiances fight the semantic colours.** Grove's green primary is 2° off `success`;
   Blood's crimson is 10° off `danger`; Amber's gold is 17° off `warning` and 16° off the Arcane
   `highlight` gold. ADR 0020 step 3 asks the semantic hues to _"stand out from any primary"_; they
   cannot when the primary is the same hue. These three themes are the "garbage" ones, and the
   formula, not the hues, is why the other three merely look flat.

Two smaller systemic points: the type scale (ADR 0017) is respected and reads well; the layout
(ADR 0022) holds, no text is cut, and the 1707 viewport shows everything. The problems are colour,
tone and emphasis, not type or geometry, with the exceptions noted per screen.

## Per-screen findings

Each finding: what is wrong, why it matters at the table, where it comes from. Screenshot names are
in `shots/`.

### Shell and header (`dark-arcane--act-weapons-1707x1300`, `header-effects-expanded`, `header-turn-menu`)

- **The header card is the same colour as the page.** On a dark screen the eye finds the header by
  its content, not its box. The brand mark is a 1 px outline in `tint`, the wordmark in muted-ish
  Cinzel: the product name has less weight than the word "TURN" next to it.
- **The turn number competes with nothing.** `text-hero` "1" is the biggest glyph on the screen,
  but the initiative value (the number the player needs at the start of each turn) is a tiny
  `bg-amb-900` chip at 11 px; same chip style as the "CURRENT" tag in the journal and the "+2"
  expander. Three different meanings, one chip.
- **The effects timeline is the best-designed element** (clear pills, dimmed past, warning on the
  last turn), but its label column is a fixed `max-w` that wraps "Protection From Evil" onto two
  lines at 1440 while the track beside it has 600 px of empty cells (`header-effects-expanded`).
  With more than 3 effects the header is 190 px tall when expanded and pushes the stage; the "+N"
  expander is a chevron the size of a letter.
- **The last result is docked right but reads as a footnote**: an 11 px uppercase label, a chip and
  one line of body text at 70 %. It is the one thing the DM asks the player to read aloud. At 1440
  and in French it wraps onto two lines (`dark-arcane-fr--act-weapons-1440x900`).
- **The ⋯ menu** (`header-turn-menu`) is a bare popover with three text rows and no title; the
  "End combat" destructive item looks like the others.

### Character card (`dark-arcane--act-weapons-1440x900`, `act-hp-low`, `character-details`)

- **The HP bar is painted in the ambiance hue when HP is above 50 %.** Health is the one bar whose
  colour must mean something; here it changes with the theme (violet, green, red, blue, gold, grey
  in the six `act-weapons` shots) and is the same colour as the Attack button and the rail pill.
  In Blood a healthy bar is red. Below 25 % it turns `danger` and the "Badly wounded" label
  appears in `warning` (`act-hp-low`): the bar and its caption disagree in colour.
- **The seven ability cells** are `bg-neu-900/60` tiles (1.1:1 off the card), THAC0 `bg-neu-800`;
  the labels are 11 px at 70 %. The row reads, but it has no anchor; the character's name (`text-title`,
  14 px) is smaller than the turn number and the XP total elsewhere.
- **"HP CHANGE"**: an unlabelled 56 px field with placeholder "HP", two outlined buttons whose red
  and green text is the only colour in the card, and a placeholder-only "Source" field. The two
  placeholders are the labels; once typed into, the fields have none.
- **Saving throws** are five outlined rows whose outline is 1.6:1 off the card: they look like
  disabled inputs, not buttons ("CLICK TO ROLL" in the label is compensating for that). The last
  roll "✓ 15" / "✗ 8" is the right idea and the only place a verdict is iconic.
- **Below the saves, 40 % of the card is empty at 1707** (ADR 0022 step 2.3 accepts this); at 1440 it
  is full. The emptiness is fine; the lack of grouping tone is not: identity, HP and saves are three
  groups separated by 24 px of nothing on the same flat surface.
- **Character details** (`character-details`): a popover of nine "Not set" rows in 70 % ink. The
  popover has a real title and shadow and is the most legible panel on the screen, which says
  something about the surfaces behind it.
- **French**: the save names wrap to two lines ("Bâton/Sceptre/Baguette", "Pétrification/Métamorphose")
  and the saves label wraps ("JETS DE SAUVEGARDE · CLIQUER POUR LANCER"); the card grows by about
  60 px and the rhythm of the five rows is lost (`dark-arcane-fr--lookup-1707x1300`).

### Act · Weapons (`act-weapons-1440x900`, `act-weapons-bow`, `act-weapons-crit-table`)

- **The act panel has no slack at 1440x900.** The now band is capped at 60 % of the stage; with the
  header at rest the panel's content (target bar, weapon in hand, chips, crit table) fits to the
  pixel, and as soon as the header grows (effects expanded, `header-effects-expanded`) or the
  target bar wraps, the crit table row is cut at the bottom edge behind a 2 px scrollbar, while the
  result card beside it is two-thirds empty and the journal below has eight rows. The space is
  there; the split puts it in the wrong box.
- **Target bar**: `bg-neu-900` at 1.19:1; "TARGET", "AC", "Size", "Situational" in three different
  label styles (uppercase section label, muted body, sentence body). The AC stepper's field is
  `bg-background` (the page colour), i.e. darker than its card: every input in the app is a hole.
- **Weapon in hand**: the one row the tab is about has a `border-amb-700 bg-amb-900/40` tint that is
  measurable but not visible (the tint vs the card is about 1.1:1). The Attack button is the only
  element in the whole screen with a saturated fill; it pops because nothing else does. Its second
  line "needs 6 vs AC 5" is 11 px at 80 % opacity on the fill: the most useful number in the tab is
  the hardest to read.
- **Switch-to chips**: outlined like the saves, the dice in muted text. Ammunition chips
  (`act-weapons-bow`) are filled `bg-amb-900 text-tint` for the chosen one and outlined for the
  others: the same visual as "selected journal row" and "active rail item". The count turns
  `warning` at 3 or less, which is good.
- **Crit table** (`act-weapons-crit-table`): a `<details>` with a muted summary; opened, it is a
  `bg-neu-900/40` block of three inputs per range, the only place the app uses a native `summary`
  disclosure beside "Calculation details" and "Spell text", each styled differently.

### Result card (`act-weapons-*`, `act-save-result`, `act-spells-cast`, `act-journal-selected`)

- **The result card is the latest result, outlined in the accent (ADR 0022 step 4.1).** Measured,
  that outline is 2.49:1 (dark) / 1.59:1 (light) off the card, 1 px wide: it is not distinguishable
  from the other cards' outline without looking for it. The "Latest ↑" button appears when an older
  entry is shown and is the only way to tell the two states apart.
- **The hexagon d20** in `tint` with dark text is a strong element; the verdict "Hit" / "Passed" is
  `text-success` at 16 px, right-aligned, the same weight as the "Hits AC 1" headline on the left.
  The total damage in `highlight` gold `text-hero` is the right call and the one thing that pops in
  the card. In Amber the gold becomes violet and the max die chip becomes dark grey: the "crit /
  max" code is theme-dependent (`dark-amber--act-weapons`).
- **The correction forms** ("Target AC − 5 + Apply", "Adjust after the roll") are `bg-neu-900/60`
  blocks with a 28 px input and a disabled-looking `Apply` (it is `bg-primary` at 50 % opacity when
  nothing changed). The save card's three stat tiles (d20 / Target / Margin) are the clearest tile
  pattern in the app and are not reused by the attack card, which lists the same facts as text.
- **The cast card** (`act-spells-cast`): ten die chips in a row with one in gold, "= 40", then
  "Damage 40" again top right in `text-hero`. The dice are the same chip style as the ammunition
  and the "+2" badges in the inventory.
- **Selected entry** (`act-journal-selected`): a dice result with a "Reroll" text link in the card
  and another on the hovered row; the card has 80 % empty height at 1707.

### Journal (`act-weapons-1707x1300`, `act-journal-collapsed`, `act-spells-cast`)

- **Undone entries dominate.** A struck-through row keeps its icon and gets a red "Undone" tag; after
  a few corrections the current turn is a stack of red tags and strikethroughs (`act-skills`, light
  `act-spells-cast`). Undone is history, not an alarm; red is the alarm colour.
- **The value column** uses `highlight` gold for every roll total and red / green for HP. Gold means
  "crit / max" in the result card and "any roll" here: the secondary accent carries two codes.
- **Current turn**: a `bg-amb-900/…` tint under the group and a `tint` bar at the left of the
  selected row, plus a "CURRENT" chip; three marks for two states. The tint is 1.13:1 off the card.
- **The time column** is 11 px at 70 % and takes a 60 px column on every row at the far right of a
  1300 px table; the entry text starts at a fixed 250 px indent so the "OUT OF COMBAT" label fits.
  At 1707 that leaves 900 px of mostly empty line per entry.
- **Collapsed bar** (`act-journal-collapsed`): good; the one place where the turn tag, value, icon,
  text and time read as one line.

### Act · Spells tab (`act-spells`, `light-arcane--act-spells-cast`)

- **"Cast" is a secondary grey chip**, 24 px, the same `bg-neu-900` as the level pips' background,
  while "Prepare spells" and "Long Rest (Reset)" are outlined 28 px buttons on top. The primary action
  of the tab is the least emphasised control in it; "Used" is muted text. The level groups flow in two
  text columns (ADR 0022 step 3.2) and that works.
- **Slots**: "2 / 3 prepared ● ● 2 / 2": two numbers and pips for one fact. The filled pip is
  `tint`, the empty `neu-700`; at 6 px they are the smallest meaningful mark on the screen.
- **The damage line** ("1d3+20 fire", "5 × 1d4+1 force") is 11 px muted under the name; it is what
  the player compares spells by.

### Act · Items and Skills (`act-items`, `act-skills`)

- **Items**: a four-column grid with "Roll" / "Use" as `bg-neu-900` chips, dice as muted text; no
  row separation at all (1.0:1, nothing drawn), and the tab's panel shrinks to its content so the
  band is 230 px tall with a result card beside it of the same height.
- **Skills**: two `bg-neu-900` blocks (ADR 0022 step 2.5), column headers in uppercase labels, and
  a "− 0 +" adjustment stepper in the block's header with no label. Rows are buttons with no
  affordance until hover. The block scrolls at 1440 inside the 60 % band.

### Spells activity, the lookup (`lookup-1707x1300`, `lookup-long-1440x900`)

- **The stage is mostly empty card.** The list opens on the first Wizard spell ("Alarm", a
  one-line description), so at 1707 the detail card is 1300 x 1000 px with 200 px of content at the
  top (`lookup-1707x1300`); while the catalogue loads, the list is empty and the detail says
  "Select a spell to see it here." in muted text. The two cards have the same outline as everything
  else, so the stage reads as one blank area with a search box in the corner.
- **Filters**: a search input, a native `<select>` ("Any school", browser-styled), a segmented
  control (`bg-neu-900` track, `bg-amb-900 text-tint` thumb: the fourth use of that pair), and ten
  level chips as outlined circles. Four control styles in 120 px of height.
- **The list** is the second-best element of the app: level in `magic`, name in body, selected row
  with a tint bar. **The detail** is the best: name in `text-headline`, a meta line, stat tiles on
  `bg-neu-900` with the raw book text dimmed under the computed value, a cast box, the description
  in two text columns. Its weakness is tone: tiles at 1.19:1 off the card, and "AT LEVEL 15 · No dice
  to roll" taking a full-width raised box for a no-op.
- **French**: filter chips and tabs translate well; the list and detail are unchanged.

### Inventory activity (`inventory-1707x1300`, `inventory-item-form-1440x900`)

- **Two cards, "Items" and "Weapons", with `text-title` headings**, while every other card on the
  screen is headed by an uppercase `section-label`. This is the most visible of the two heading
  systems (see Consistency below).
- **Rows** alternate `bg-neu-900/40`-ish stripes at about 1.1:1: visible on a 2560 monitor, gone
  on a laptop. The "+2" bonus badge is a tiny `bg-amb-900 text-tint` chip (fifth use); the dice chip
  is outlined with a dice icon (a sixth chip style); the quantity stepper is two outlined circles
  around a number; the row actions are two bare icons at 70 %.
- **Add item form** (`inventory-item-form`): eight fields in a `bg-neu-900`-less area at the top of
  the card, labels 11 px muted, inputs `bg-background` (page colour, darker than the card), a filled
  "Add" and an outlined "Cancel". Form fields across the app look like cut-outs in dark mode because
  they are darker than their surface; in light mode they are tinted lavender / green / pink by the
  ambiance (`light-grove`, `light-blood`), which reads as "invalid" or "disabled".

### Experience activity (`experience-1707x1300`, `light-arcane--experience`)

- **The XP total in `text-hero`** with "Before level 16 · 26,582 XP" in gold, a tint progress bar
  with 10 % ticks and eight level cells: the best use of hierarchy in the app, because the number the
  card is about is the biggest thing in it and the secondary accent is used once.
- **The level cells**: current is `bg-amb-900` with a tint outline, next is outlined in `border`,
  the others `bg-neu-900`. Three fills for three states at 1.1–1.2:1 apart; the current level is
  told apart by the outline alone.
- **The ledger card**: "LEDGER" and column headers in section labels, then the empty state "No XP
  recorded yet." and the sheet's starting row in muted text. At 1707, 1000 px of empty card.
- **"Add XP"**: five controls in 24rem; the "+10 %" checkbox is the native control in `accent-tint`
  and the Add button is `bg-primary` at 50 % until an amount is typed.

### Popovers (`settings`, `header-add-effect`, `character-details`, `header-turn-menu`)

- Popovers carry `shadow-float` and a `neu-600` ring and are the only elements that visibly sit
  above their surroundings. The settings popover is well organised (three labelled radio groups,
  ambiance swatches in their own hue). The add-effect popover opens on a tabbed form with a
  disabled-looking "Activate" (`bg-primary/50` until valid) next to a text "Cancel".

### Dice footer (`every shot`)

- Seven outlined dice chips, a formula input, "− +", "Clear", a star, a filled "Roll" and "No
  favourite yet": the footer is a toolbar with five control styles on one line. The chosen die's
  count badge ("2 −") sits half outside its chip. "Roll" is the second saturated fill on the Act
  screen, the same colour as Attack: two primary actions, one hue.

### Token-level fixes (apply to any direction)

Numbers are proposals for Arcane dark / light; the generator in `theme.ts` would carry them to the
other ambiances. Each is one line in `themeVars` (ADR 0020 step 2's table changes accordingly).

| Token | Now | Proposed | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ground` | L .15 / .93 | **L .11 / .94** | card on page 1.10 → ~1.45 (dark); the page recedes |
| `surface` | L .205 / .985 | **L .23 / .995** | with the ground change, cards read as cards |
| `neu-900` (raised step) | L .27 / .90 | **L .31 / .88** | step on card 1.19 → ~1.4; sections visible |
| `--border` | ink 18 % | **ink 32 %** (dark) / **26 %** (light) | card outline 1.56 → ~2.6; still not 3:1, but with the tone step it no longer needs to be |
| `--input` | ink 22 % | **ink 40 %**, fields on `surface` not `background` | inputs stop being holes |
| `ink-muted` | ink 70 % | **ink 78 %** dark / **72 %** light, and used for *meta only* (times, placeholders, hints) | values and names return to full ink |
| `tint` (dark) | L .72 C .14 | **L .66 C .13** | the chrome steps back half a stop so the accents below can step forward |
| `highlight` (dark) | L .82 | **L .88** | gold is the brightest thing: crits and totals pop |
| `danger` / `success` (dark) | L .72 / .78 | **L .70 C .18 / L .80 C .16**; light **L .48 / .46** | red darker and more saturated, green brighter: 3:1 apart from each other and ≥ 1.8:1 from tint |
| `warning` | hue 55 | **hue 60, L .84** | a stop above danger, below highlight |
| `amb-700` result outline | 1 px | **2 px in `tint`** or a 4 px left bar | "latest" is marked, not hinted |
| Selection (`amb-900`) | 1.13 | **`amb-800` + a 3 px `tint` bar** | one mark for "selected", used only for selection |

A new unit-test rule would keep this: every accent at least **1.8:1 off `tint`**, `surface` at least
**1.4:1 off `ground`**, `neu-900` at least **1.3:1 off `surface`**, plus the existing AA text checks.

## Recommendations

Four directions, ranked. Each is a brief a mock-up builder can act on against the current app on
the real tokens (`docs/design/ui-directions/` has the tooling: static pages from `web/src/index.css`
tokens, screenshots at both viewports, the no-cut audit). They are not exclusive: 1 is the token
floor every other direction stands on; 2, 3 and 4 differ in how far they go and which ADR steps
they touch. All keep ADR 0022's layout (cards, no rules, the split journal, the 44 px Attack),
ADR 0017's type roles and ADR 0020's three-palette model unless stated.

### 1. Contrast ladder (ranked first: smallest change, largest effect)

- **Goal.** Make the existing design visible: cards separate from the page, sections from cards,
  and the six accents sit on a lightness ladder so emphasis is read before hue.
- **Palette / token strategy.** The token table above, unchanged hues. Order by lightness in dark
  mode: ground .11 < surface .23 < raised .31 < `tint` .66 < `danger` .70 < `success` .80 <
  `warning` .84 < `highlight` .88 < ink .95. Light mode mirrors it with ink .18, `tint` .42,
  `danger` .48, `success` .46, `highlight` .58 (gold text) and `highlight-fill` .80. `ink-muted`
  for meta text only; numbers, names and verdicts in full ink.
- **Separation strategy.** Tone first, outline second: the page darker, every card lighter, raised
  blocks one more step; outlines at 32 %. One selection mark (a 3 px `tint` bar + `amb-800`), one
  "latest" mark (a 2 px `tint` outline on the result card), one chip family (filled `neu-900` for
  neutral chips, `tint`-outlined for the chosen one, `highlight-fill` only for crit / max).
- **Per screen.** Header: brand mark 2 px stroke, initiative as a `text-key` number not a chip, the
  last result line in full ink `text-body` with its value chip in the verdict colour. Character
  card: HP bar always semantic (success / warning / danger), ability tiles raised a full step, save
  rows filled `neu-900` with the target in `text-key`. Weapons: target bar raised, weapon in hand on
  `amb-800` with a 2 px `tint` outline, Attack unchanged, the "needs N" line at 100 %. Result: 2 px
  outline, stat tiles for the attack as for the save, verdict word in `text-key` filled pill
  (`success` / `danger` with `on-accent`). Journal: undone rows in muted ink with a muted "undone"
  tag (no red), the gold value only on crits and maxima, roll totals in ink. Spells tab: Cast as a
  28 px `tint` outlined button, "Used" struck. Lookup / Inventory / XP: tiles and row stripes at the
  new raised step, inputs on surface with 40 % borders, headings unified to `section-label`.
- **Trade-offs.** No new idea: the app looks the same, legible. The six ambiances stay; Grove and
  Blood are still wrong (the ladder separates by lightness, so they become *usable*, not good). Light
  mode's tinted inputs remain unless fields move to `surface` (included above).
- **ADR touchpoints.** ADR 0020 step 2's value table (new lightness per role), step 4 adds the three
  non-text rules; ADR 0022 step 4.1's "accent outline" becomes 2 px. No layout change.

### 2. Neutral chrome, colour means something (ranked second)

- **Goal.** Stop the ambiance hue from being wallpaper. The chrome (buttons, tabs, rail, bars,
  selection, borders) is drawn in neutral ink tones; colour appears only where it carries meaning:
  danger, success, warning, magic, the gold of crits, and the brand mark. The theme's hue tints the
  neutrals faintly and colours the brand, the focus ring and the one primary action in view.
- **Palette / token strategy.** Direction 1's ladder plus: `tint` keeps its hue but is used as a
  *fill* only for Attack / Cast / Roll / Next turn (the four primary actions) and the focus ring;
  everywhere else "primary" maps to `neu-700`/`neu-800` (selected row, rail pill, segmented thumb,
  weapon in hand, initiative chip, current turn, progress bars). The HP bar is semantic. Effect pills
  use `magic` for spells and `neu-600` for mundane effects, `warning` on the last turn. Ambiance
  swatches become "chrome tint + brand colour", so even Grove and Blood work because their hue no
  longer paints bars and values, only the mark, the ring and four buttons (`dark-obsidian` is the
  proof of concept: look how red, green and gold read there).
- **Separation strategy.** Direction 1's tones. Because chrome is grey, a raised block can be a
  shade lighter without competing with any accent; sections are told by tone, and a single filled
  `section-label` bar (full-width `neu-900`, 24 px) heads each group inside a card, replacing the
  floating uppercase label.
- **Per screen.** Header: timeline pills neutral / magic, current column on `neu-900` with a `tint`
  top bar; the last result's value chip in the verdict colour. Character card: tiles neutral,
  HP semantic, saves as neutral filled rows with the verdict icon in colour. Weapons: target bar and
  weapon-in-hand neutral; Attack the only `tint`. Result card: neutral tiles, verdict pill in colour,
  total in gold. Journal: value column coloured only for HP, pass / fail and crits; rolls in ink;
  current turn `neu-900`. Lookup: level numbers stay `magic` (the one coloured column), selected row
  `neu-800`. Inventory: bonus badges `highlight` text, no violet chips. XP: bar neutral with the
  current level cell in `tint` outline.
- **Trade-offs.** Less "brand" on every screen: Arcane's violet shows in the mark, the ring, four
  buttons and the faint tint of every grey. Players who liked the coloured chrome lose it. The
  ambiance picker becomes less dramatic (which is the point).
- **ADR touchpoints.** ADR 0020 step 1 (the primary palette's role: "chrome, selection, actions"
  becomes "the four primary actions, the ring, the mark"); step 2's table gains `neu-*` as the
  selection and chrome roles; ADR 0009 step 4's near-neutral rule generalises.

### 3. Raised stage (ranked third: structure, for "sections are hard to see")

- **Goal.** Three visible elevation levels instead of one: the page, the cards, and *the one card
  the player is acting in*, which rises a step and gets a filled header band. Inside cards, every
  group has a header bar, not a floating label.
- **Palette / token strategy.** Direction 1's ladder plus an `elevated` surface (dark L .27, light
  .999 with a 2 px outline) and a `band` token (dark `neu-900`, light `neu-900`) for header bars.
  Hue rules as today or as direction 2.
- **Separation strategy.** Every card has a 28 px header band on `band` holding its label (section
  label, left) and its controls (right: tabs, filters, Undo / Redo, Add); the body sits below on
  `surface`. The focused card (act panel in Act, the list in Lookup, Items in Inventory, Progress in
  XP) is on `elevated`. Groups inside a card use a 20 px sub-band. This keeps ADR 0022 step 4.1
  (no rules: the band is a tone, not a line) and replaces step 4.2's floating labels with labels in
  bands.
- **Per screen.** Header: the brand in its own band at the left edge, turn / initiative / effects /
  last result as four banded cells. Character card: three bands (NAME · LEVEL, HIT POINTS, SAVING
  THROWS). Act: tabs sit *in* the act panel's band; the result card's band shows the kind tag, name
  and time; the journal's band holds the filter, count and Undo / Redo. Lookup: filters in the list
  card's band, the detail's name in its band. Inventory and XP: the headings become bands, so the
  `text-title` vs `section-label` split disappears by construction.
- **Trade-offs.** Costs 28 px per card (about 56 px of stage height at 1440x900: the Weapons panel
  then scrolls one more row unless the 60 % cap moves to 65 %). More visual weight; closest to a
  classic VTT look. Light mode bands need care (a grey band on white).
- **ADR touchpoints.** ADR 0022 step 3.2 (card padding: a band replaces the top padding), step 4.1
  (an elevated third tone), step 4.2 (labels in bands); ADR 0017 step 6 (header budget: bands fit in
  120 px only if the turn cells share one band).

### 4. Three bespoke themes (ranked fourth: identity, more work)

- **Goal.** Replace six formula-generated ambiances with three hand-tuned themes that are each
  *designed*, with the semantic colours chosen per theme so they always stand out: **Arcane**
  (dark, the brand), **Parchment** (light, from Amber light: warm paper, brown ink, oxblood danger,
  moss success, gilt highlight), **Deep** (dark, from Ocean: ink blue, coral danger, jade success).
  Obsidian stays as "Plain" for people who want no colour.
- **Palette / token strategy.** `themeVars` keeps computing ramps, but the role table (ADR 0020
  step 2) becomes per-theme literals, including the semantic hues: Parchment's danger is hue 20 at
  L .45 C .17 on paper, not the dark-mode red flipped. Each theme ships with its own `highlight`
  chosen as the *complement in lightness* of its primary (dark primary → bright gilt; bright primary
  → deep gold text). Direction 1's non-text rules are enforced per theme.
- **Separation strategy.** Direction 1's tones, tuned per theme (Parchment uses a visible 1 px
  brown outline at 30 % because paper has no tone depth).
- **Per screen.** As direction 1; Parchment additionally gives the result card a gilt outline and
  the brand mark a brown stroke, and uses Cinzel small caps for the section labels (one more use of
  the display face, which ADR 0020 step 5 would have to allow).
- **Trade-offs.** Drops Grove, Blood, Amber dark and Ocean light: users lose choices; the settings
  popover shows three or four swatches. Most design effort of the four. Highest payoff in "this is
  Dungeons and Dragons, not a spreadsheet".
- **ADR touchpoints.** ADR 0009 step 4's ambiance list and the "near-neutral" rule; ADR 0020 step 1
  (three palettes per ambiance → a role table per theme), step 3 (semantic hues per theme, not
  global), step 5 (the display face in section labels, Parchment only); the i18n names.

### Which to pick

Direction 1 is the floor and can ship alone. The user's words point at 1 + 3 ("sections are hard to
see") or 1 + 2 ("things don't pop", "inconsistencies everywhere": one colour per meaning removes
most of the chip and tone variants). Direction 4 is where the palette question ends up if the
answer to "which themes are usable" is to be "all of them". A mock-up round should show, at
1707x1300 and 1440x900 in dark and light: 1 alone, 1 + 2, 1 + 3, and 4's Parchment, on the Act /
Weapons screen after an attack and on the lookup.
